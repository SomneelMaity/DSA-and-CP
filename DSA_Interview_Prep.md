## 3. Study Plan

### Phase 1 — Warm-up (Weeks 1–2)
Revisit fundamentals. Solve Easy problems in every category.
- Arrays, Hashing, Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Stack
- Target: ~5 problems/day

### Phase 2 — Core Strengthening (Weeks 3–6)
Focus on Medium problems. Complete full topic blocks.
- Trees, Graphs, Binary Search, Linked Lists, Backtracking, Heaps
- Target: ~4–5 problems/day

### Phase 3 — Hard & Company-Specific (Weeks 7–10)
- DP (1D & 2D), Advanced Graphs, Tries
- Go through your target company section above
- Target: ~3–4 problems/day with solution reviews

### Phase 4 — Mock Interviews (Weeks 11–12)
- Use [LeetCode Contest](https://leetcode.com/contest/) for timed practice
- Use [Pramp](https://www.pramp.com) or [interviewing.io](https://interviewing.io) for mock interviews
- Revisit all Hard problems you've attempted
